# Model v7: learned from several monsoons

Seasons: 2010 (NASA, 42 events), 2011 (NASA, 10 events), 2012 (NASA, 11 events), 2013 (NASA, 45 events), 2015 (NASA, 18 events), 2026 (PWD, 450 events).
Each season held out in turn. Share of reported landslides / closures caught when each method flags the same share of stretch-days:

| Method | NASA years, 10% of days | NASA years, 25% | PWD 2026, 10% | PWD 2026, 25% |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rain threshold engine (Caine, GSI) | 36% | 67% | 26% | 48% |
| v7 logistic regression | 25% | 58% | 24% | 52% |
| v7 gradient-boosted trees | 33% | 64% | 13% | 35% |
