# Check on other years

Dated landslides near PahaadPath hill roads from NASA catalogues (COOLR; GLC for years before 2018), June to September, scored with rain and soil moisture of each day. Neither method was fitted on these years.

Seasons: 2010, 2011, 2012, 2013, 2015. Stretch-days with a reported landslide: 126.

| Method | Caught at Caution | Days flagged Caution | Caught at Avoid | Days flagged Avoid | Caught when 10% of days are flagged | Caught when 25% are flagged |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rain threshold engine (Caine, GSI) | 35% | 9% | 17% | 2% | 37% | 69% |
| Model v6 (trained on 2026 PWD closures) | 56% | 35% | 10% | 8% | 13% | 36% |

Model v6 catches fewer than the engine at the same share of days flagged: the app uses the engine.

News catalogues miss most landslides, so days without one are not known to be safe: compare the catch rates, especially at the same share of days flagged.
