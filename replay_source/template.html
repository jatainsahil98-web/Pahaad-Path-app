PahaadPath closure replay

Runs the prototype's own landslide engine (copied unchanged from PahaadPath_prototype.html) on recorded Open-Meteo rain for days when hill roads closed, then compares Avoid stretches with closures named in the news. Needs an internet connection. No fresh reports are used, so only rain, slope class and incident history drive the score. Slope and incident values are still the demo placeholders.

How to read the numbers

  • Hit rate: stretches with a named closure that the engine scored Avoid (65 or more) at any hour from 00:00 to 23:00 IST on that day, divided by all stretches with a named closure.
  • Avoid with no named closure: news counts (about 150 roads on 20 Aug 2025, 195 on 9 Jul 2026) include many link roads that are not named and not on our map. So these flags are an upper bound on false alarms, not a false-alarm ratio.
  • Control days are dry-season days. Any Avoid there is a false alarm.
  • Recorded rain is a gridded reanalysis at each link's midpoint. It shows whether the threshold logic would have fired with good rain data. The "issued 1 or 2 days before" sources test real lead time.
